Piano Model X

A physically modelled piano: every string is an individual waveguide with its own geometry, tension and losses, and they all meet at a shared bridge — so unison beating and sympathetic resonance happen on their own rather than being effects.

Connect a MIDI keyboard before or after starting. No MIDI? Play with the computer keyboard (z…m, q…u) or click the keys. space is the sustain pedal.

Hear the sympathetic resonance: shift-click C4 to hold it silently (its dampers lift but no hammer strikes), then play C3 hard and release. C4 keeps ringing — driven entirely through the bridge.

Instrument

Coupling is expressed as the fraction of each string's own loss that goes into the bridge instead of into internal damping — so it can never destabilise, and the decay time you ask for stays the decay time you get. Unison coupling drives beating and double decay; bridge coupling drives the sympathetic halo.

Strike position is a fraction of the speaking length; it notches out every partial with a node there (1/8 kills the 8th). That notch is why measured partials 8–9 nearly vanish — the physics, not a bug.
